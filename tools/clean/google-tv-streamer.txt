# Google TV Streamer (2024, kirkwood, Android 14 Google TV): what AwanTV-clean.ps1 disables.
# From CleanYourTV (github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv, MIT): examples/google-tv-streamer-4k-kirkwood, checked on a real
# unit, and the two lines most of its other lists share (partner attribution in 76 lists, calendar sync in 63).
# A Google device has no maker's ads or tracking, so the list is short.
# Unlike CleanYourTV, Awan TV keeps the stock launcher (launcherx), the screensaver (dreamx) and setup (setupwraith):
# on this device setupwraith also holds the Cast credentials and owns the saved Wi-Fi network, and disabling it
# broke casting and can drop Wi-Fi. The Privacy Sandbox modules are in extra-google-tv-streamer.txt.
# One package a line; "#" starts a comment. Disabled for the user (pm disable-user), never uninstalled.
