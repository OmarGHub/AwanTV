# Chromecast with Google TV (4K 2020 "sabrina", HD 2022 "boreal"; Google TV 14): what AwanTV-clean.ps1 disables.
# From CleanYourTV (github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv, MIT): examples/google-chromecast-gtv-4k/debloat-keep-cast.txt
# ("lived with" on a real unit: stock home, phone casting, Play Store and Assistant all worked) and
# examples/google-chromecast-gtv-hd (its auto-install stub), plus the two lines most of its other lists share (partner attribution, calendar sync).
# Awan TV keeps the stock launcher (launcherx), the screensaver (dreamx, backdrop) and setup (setupwraith): on this
# device setupwraith holds the Cast receiver's credentials and can own the saved Wi-Fi network, so disabling it breaks
# casting and can drop Wi-Fi. The Privacy Sandbox modules and the optional apps are in extra-google-chromecast-gtv.txt.
# Packages a unit does not ship (netoscope on the HD; the auto-install stub was listed on the HD only) are skipped.
# One package a line; "#" starts a comment. Disabled for the user (pm disable-user), never uninstalled.

# Telemetry and feedback
com.google.android.feedback
com.android.tv.feedbackconsent
com.google.android.tv.remotecontrol.logging     # remote usage logging
com.google.android.apps.tv.netoscope            # network diagnostics reporting (4K only)

# Preloads and partner set-up
com.google.android.play.games
com.android.printspooler                        # printing; no printer on a TV
com.google.android.partnersetup                 # partner-app attribution
com.google.android.syncadapters.calendar        # Google Calendar sync; no calendar on a TV
android.autoinstalls.config.google.gtvpai       # Play Auto Installs stub (installs partner apps unasked; on the HD)
